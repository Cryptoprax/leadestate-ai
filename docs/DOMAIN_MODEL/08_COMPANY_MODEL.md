# Company Model

Company represents a legal or operating organization such as agency, developer, brokerage, landlord, vendor, lender, insurer, or corporate buyer. It owns legal identifiers, tax identifiers, addresses, contact relationships, hierarchy, and classifications.

Company *↔* Contact through role/validity relationships. Company 1→* developments/properties where acting as developer or owner. Company *↔* Deal through participant roles. Parent/subsidiary and partner relationships are effective-dated and typed. Organization is the SaaS tenant; Company is business-party data and must never be conflated with tenancy.
