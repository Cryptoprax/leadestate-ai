# Enterprise Marketing Assets and Interactive Demo

Sprint 69 extends the existing public website, Aurora demo workspace, documentation platform, and marketing observability. It does not introduce another demo or AI runtime.

Industry, comparison, sales, customer, media, and investor pages are composed from a shared marketing catalog through repository and service boundaries. Comparison claims explicitly require direct vendor verification. Customer stories remain templates until evidence and publication approval exist. The ROI calculator shows its assumptions and produces directional estimates only.

The live guided demo remains isolated, deterministic, read-only, and recommendation-governed. Marketing telemetry stores sanitized route and event metadata through the existing hashed-session RPC; it never records demo fixture payloads, CRM data, or secrets.
