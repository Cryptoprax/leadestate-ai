# AtlasOS Roadmap

## Roadmap policy

Versions represent capability milestones rather than fixed dates. Each version
must meet security, tenancy, accessibility, observability, documentation, and
operational readiness gates before general availability.

## Version 1 — Foundation

Establish architecture boundaries, design system, configuration conventions,
tenant context contracts, permission vocabulary, auditing schema, monitoring
baseline, delivery standards, and Master Blueprint governance.

**Exit outcome:** A production-ready platform skeleton with validated standards
and no product-specific contamination of the core.

## Version 2 — Mission Control

Deliver the internal command center foundation: Executive Dashboard,
organization and user visibility, monitoring, support context, security views,
system settings, and privileged-action controls.

**Exit outcome:** Authorized operators can understand platform state and perform
essential governed administration.

## Version 3 — Authentication

Implement identity lifecycle, secure sign-in, sessions, recovery, MFA,
invitations, service identities, and an enterprise federation path.

**Exit outcome:** Identities authenticate securely with observable, revocable
sessions and strong privileged-access controls.

## Version 4 — Organizations

Implement organization lifecycle, country/region/branch/department/team
hierarchy, memberships, role hierarchy, scoped RBAC, tenant configuration,
provisioning, suspension, and isolation verification.

**Exit outcome:** Multiple organizations operate safely with delegated
administration and explicit tenant boundaries.

## Version 5 — CRM

Release the installable CRM foundation with contacts, leads, pipelines,
activities, assignments, configurable fields and views, permissions, events,
imports, data quality, and analytics.

**Exit outcome:** Vayon OS can run core customer operations on a reusable
CRM module.

## Version 6 — AI

Deliver the provider gateway, AI employee registry, knowledge, memory, tools,
budgets, evaluations, monitoring, safety, and initial Sales and Support AI
employees.

**Exit outcome:** Governed AI employees perform measurable work within explicit
permissions and human oversight.

## Version 7 — Messaging

Deliver normalized conversations, channels, templates, shared inbox, consent,
delivery pipeline, AI/human handoff, provider health, and messaging analytics.

**Exit outcome:** Products communicate reliably across approved channels through
one governed platform.

## Version 8 — Automation

Release Workflow Builder, typed actions, triggers, durable execution, approvals,
retries, schedules, operational controls, and workflow analytics.

**Exit outcome:** Organizations automate cross-module work with visible state,
permission safety, and recoverable failure.

## Version 9 — Marketplace

Launch publisher onboarding, module packaging, review, listings, consented
installation, compatibility, metering, billing integration, runtime governance,
and lifecycle management.

**Exit outcome:** Approved third parties can extend AtlasOS safely through stable
contracts.

## Version 10 — AtlasOS Builder

Complete Builder Studio with Navigation, Dashboard, Page, Module, Workflow,
Form, Report, Permission, Theme, Email, and Notification builders; add product
manifests, previews, approvals, staged publication, and rollback.

**Exit outcome:** AtlasOS can configure and launch new vertical SaaS products
from governed metadata and reusable modules without codebase forks.

## Cross-version workstreams

Security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, developer experience, billing,
analytics, data governance, localization, performance, compliance readiness,
documentation, and cost efficiency advance in every version.
