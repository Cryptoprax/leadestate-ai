# Marketing Platform

## Purpose

The Marketing Platform is a multi-product, multi-site content and campaign
system. It enables marketing teams to publish accessible, high-performing
experiences while preserving brand governance, consent, attribution, security,
and auditability.

## Landing Pages

Teams compose responsive pages from approved design-system sections. Pages
support drafts, reusable blocks, localization, personalization rules, forms,
experiments, preview, approval, scheduling, domains, redirects, and rollback.

## SEO

SEO capabilities include titles, descriptions, canonicals, robots policy,
sitemaps, structured data, social cards, redirect management, link validation,
performance checks, and search-console integration. Publication prevents
conflicting canonicals and accidental indexing of private environments.

## Blog

The blog supports authors, categories, tags, editorial workflow, scheduling,
related content, localization, revisions, previews, RSS, and structured article
metadata.

## Social Media

Social tools manage channel accounts, content calendars, approved assets,
campaign links, scheduled posts, review workflow, publishing outcomes, and
engagement reporting. External permissions are minimal and revocable.

## Announcements

Announcements target products, plans, regions, organizations, roles, or versions
through banners, modals, inbox items, and release notes. Frequency, expiry,
acknowledgement, accessibility, and emergency withdrawal are configurable.

## Help Center and Knowledge Base

The Help Center publishes searchable, categorized, localized support content.
The Knowledge Base maintains internal and customer-facing sources with access
policy, ownership, freshness review, versioning, feedback, and AI retrieval
eligibility.

## Email Campaigns and Newsletter

Campaigns manage audiences, consent, segmentation, templates, tests, schedules,
frequency caps, unsubscribe, suppression, delivery, bounce handling, and
attribution. Newsletters use the same governed identity and preference model.

## Referral Program

Referral capabilities include programs, links, attribution windows, eligibility,
fraud controls, milestones, rewards, status, terms, and financial reconciliation.

## Analytics integrations

The platform centrally governs Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, LinkedIn Insight,
and Microsoft Clarity by site, region, consent category, environment, and
version. No tracker loads before applicable consent. Sensitive fields are
excluded from payloads.

## Media Library

The library supports images, video, documents, metadata, rights, ownership,
folders, variants, optimization, accessibility text, usage references,
retention, and duplicate detection.

## Governance

Content roles separate authoring, review, legal approval, and publication.
Every published artifact has an owner, version, environment, effective dates,
audit trail, rollback, and measurable performance.
