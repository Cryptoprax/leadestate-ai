# Sprint 72 — World-Class Positioning and Conversion Experience

Sprint 72 establishes Vayon as the AI operating system built specifically for real estate. It is a marketing presentation change only; authentication, OAuth, APIs, providers, database schema, Supabase, RBAC, tenant isolation, and backend services are unchanged.

## Conversion narrative

The public experience now answers five buyer questions directly:

- **Why Vayon?** One platform connects AI employees, CRM, communication, property intelligence, workflow, analytics, and governance.
- **Why now?** Real estate teams lose speed and context across disconnected subscriptions and manual handoffs.
- **Why AI?** Specialized employees qualify, prepare, summarize, match, and recommend continuously while humans retain approval authority.
- **Why real estate?** The product story follows enquiries, inventory, matching, property visits, conversations, deals, and executive revenue insight.
- **Why not a generic CRM stack?** The comparison makes the cost and operational fragmentation of separate CRM, marketing, calling, WhatsApp, automation, analytics, reporting, and approval tools explicit.

All customer marks remain placeholders. Demo metrics and product-preview values are presentation data and are not represented as production or customer evidence.

## Performance and accessibility

Sprint 71's bounded client interaction module is reused. No additional animation library, image payload, API request, or persistent state was added. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences, navigation and controls remain keyboard accessible, and schema output is escaped before rendering.
